## FAQ
### 1) Do I need to sign up before seeing savings?
No. The audit result appears first. Email capture appears only after value is shown.

### 2) How accurate are recommendations?
Recommendations are deterministic and tied to current official vendor pricing, with conservative savings logic.

### 3) Which AI tools are supported?
Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf.

### 4) Are public reports safe to share?
Yes. Public report pages remove identifying details like email and company name.

### 5) When is Credex consultation recommended?
When projected savings are high, the product surfaces Credex as the fastest route to capture those savings.
