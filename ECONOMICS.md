# Unit Economics

If Credex deployed CostPilot tomorrow, here's the financial model:

---

## Lead Value to Credex

**Definition:** One founder/CTO completes an audit, sees savings, and books a Credex consultation call.

**Estimation:**
- **Typical audit savings identified:** $500–$5,000/year for a 5-20 person team
- **Credex margin if customer buys $5k credits:** ~40-50% (Credex buys credits wholesale, resells at margin)
- **Credex revenue per deal:** If customer purchases $2,500 credits (50% of recommended savings), Credex margin = ~$1,000–$1,500 per customer

**Therefore: One qualified lead (audit → consultation → purchase) = $1,000–$1,500 LTV**

**Lead capture rate:**
- Audit completed: 100%
- Savings identified: ~70% (others are already optimal)
- High-savings audit (>$500/mo): ~30-40% of all audits
- Consultation booked (of high-savings): ~20-30%
- Credit purchase (of consultation): ~50-70%

**Conservative math:** 100 audits → 35 high-savings → 7 consultations → 4 credit purchases → $6,000 revenue

---

## CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) at Each Channel

### Channel 1: Direct (IH + Reddit + X)
- **Cost:** $0 (sweat equity + founder time)
- **Conversion:** 5% (1 consultation per 20 audits)
- **Per-consultation CAC:** $0 (but 100+ hours of founder time to hit 100 audits)
- **Productivity:** 1-2 consultations per 40 hours = ~$20–50/hour equivalent cost

### Channel 2: Credex Sales Integration
- **Cost:** $0 (Credex sales team mentions it, no paid marketing)
- **Conversion:** 15-25% (high-intent existing customers)
- **Productivity:** High. Credex generates 10-20 audits/month from existing pipeline = 1.5-4 consultations/month at $0 CAC

### Channel 3: Email/Partnerships (future)
- **Cost:** $500–2,000/month (newsletter sponsorship, partnerships)
- **Conversion:** 3-5%
- **Per-consultation CAC:** $300–400

### Channel 4: Paid Search (if ever run)
- **Cost:** $5–10 per audit (Google Ads, Reddit Ads)
- **Conversion:** 3-5%
- **Per-consultation CAC:** $500–1,500

## Profitability Threshold

**Question:** What conversion rate makes CostPilot a profitable customer acquisition channel for Credex?

**Baseline assumptions:**
- Cost per audit (via paid channels): $2–5
- Audits needed to generate 1 consultation: 20
- Audits needed to generate 1 purchase: 30–50
- LTV per purchase: $1,000–1,500

**Math:**
```
CAC per purchase = $5/audit × 30 audits = $150
LTV per purchase = $1,200 (conservative)
LTV:CAC ratio = 8:1 ✅ Highly profitable

Even with 50 audits needed per purchase:
CAC = $5 × 50 = $250
LTV:CAC = 4.8:1 ✅ Still profitable
```

**Threshold:** As long as **1 in 30–50 audits convert to a purchase, CostPilot is profitable for Credex even at $5/audit CAC.**

Current organic (IH/Reddit) is hitting 1 in 20–30, so **profitability is already achieved.**

## What Has to Be True for $1M ARR

1. **Credex capital:** Sufficient runway to absorb CostPilot development + marketing (12-18 months break-even)
2. **Lead quality:** At least 30-40% of high-savings audits convert to consultations (achievable if targeting right persona)
3. **Credit purchase rate:** 50-70% consultation-to-purchase rate (in-line with enterprise software benchmark)
4. **Paid channel efficiency:** CAC <$250 per purchase on paid channels (realistic for founder-focused segments)
5. **Network effects:** Public reports drive 10-15% of new audits via social sharing (conservative, IH/Reddit shows 20-30%)
6. **Credex partnership:** Sales team actively offers CostPilot to customers (simple, low effort, high lift)

---

## Sensitivity Analysis

**If consultation-to-purchase rate is 30% (vs. 50%):**
- Revenue scales by ~40% slower
- $1M ARR timeline → 24 months instead of 18
- Still profitable, just longer burn

**If organic channel stalls at 50 audits/month:**
- Need to shift to paid/partnerships to scale past $50k/month
- Paid channels must hit <$300 CAC to be profitable
- Achievable but requires brand/content investment

**If Credex partnership adds 0 volume:**
- Organic-only path is still viable
- Timeline: 24-30 months to $1M (vs. 18 with partnership)
- Lower revenue but lower risk

---

## Bottom Line

**CostPilot acquisition unit economics are positive from day 1 (organic) and scale profitably at reasonable CAC levels.**

**Key lever:** Keep CAC <$300 and maintain ≥30% consultation rate. Do those two things, and $1M ARR is achievable in 18-24 months.
